# Background prompts for the 2D Cartoon Stage

These prompts make **empty** painted backgrounds for the 2D Cartoon Stage: no people and no animals. The app adds the characters itself.
Use them in Gemini (Imagen / "Create image"), Google Flow, Ideogram, Leonardo, Bing Image Creator, or any image tool.

## How to use

1. Copy the **style line** and paste it at the end of every prompt, so all backgrounds look like the same cartoon.
2. Generate at **16:9** (1920×1080 or bigger). Download as JPG or PNG.
3. In the app, open **Backgrounds → + Import** (or drag the files in).
4. For each background:
   - Set **Place** and **Time**.
   - Drag the **green line** to where the feet of the front row stand, and the **blue line** to where the back row stands.
   - Adjust **Character height**.
   - For charpai, chairs or benches, click **Add seat**, then click where the feet go.
5. Make 2–4 versions of each important place: day, evening and night, plus inside and outside.

**Style line (add to every prompt):**

```
Indian 2D cartoon animation background, hand-painted digital art, soft cel shading, clean outlines, warm vivid colours, wide shot at eye level, flat open ground in the lower third for characters to stand, completely empty scene, no people, no animals, no text, no watermark, 16:9
```

**If people still appear,** add this to the end of the prompt: `empty, unpopulated, nobody in the scene`.
**For a horror look,** change "warm vivid colours" to `dark desaturated blue-green colours, eerie mood`.

## Marking places (desk, judge, witness box, bed, jail cell…)

After importing, open **Backgrounds**, select the image, pick the type in **Places in this background** and click **Add place**:

| Type | Click 1 | Click 2 | Who goes there automatically |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seat | where the feet of a sitting person go | – | people who "sit" |
| Behind desk (sits) | floor behind the desk, middle | top edge of the desk | inspector, officer, doctor, teacher, boss |
| Judge bench | behind the bench, middle | top edge of the bench | the judge |
| Witness box | floor inside the box | top edge of the box | the accused / witness |
| Behind counter (stands) | floor behind the counter | top edge of the counter | scientist, shopkeeper, cashier, doctor |
| Bed | middle of the mattress (then set the width to the bed length) | – | the patient (lies down) |
| Jail cell | floor inside the cell | where the bars should start at the top | the thief / prisoner (bars are drawn in front) |
| Blackboard spot | floor in front of the board | – | the teacher |

Everything below the top edge you click hides the person, so they look like they are really behind the desk, bench or box.

## Tips

- **Same story = same set.** A 15-minute story usually needs 5–8 places. Make them in the same session with the same style line, so the colours match.
- **Day + night of the same place.** Generate the day image first, then ask: "same image, but at night". That gives a matching pair.
- **Keep the floor empty.** The lower third must be open ground. If something blocks it (a table in front), generate again.
- **Flow / Gemini plates you already have also work.** The app crops any photo to 16:9.
- **Naming:** the AI picks backgrounds by **Place, Time and Tags**. Fill these carefully, for example place = police station, time = night, tags = lock-up, inspector.
