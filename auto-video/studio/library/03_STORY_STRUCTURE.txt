# STORY STRUCTURE — DURATION SCALING (v1.0)

AI video tools ek baar mein zyada se zyada **~6–8 second** ka clip banate hain. Isliye ek shot = average **6–8 sec**. Dialogue-heavy shots 8–10 sec tak ho sakte hain.

## 1. Duration → Scenes → Shots

| Duration | Acts | Scenes | Shots (approx) | Main characters | Locations | Batches (Phase 3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 min | 1 (Hook → Twist) | 3–5 | 8–10 | 1–2 | 1–2 | 1 (same reply) |
| 3 min | 3 | 6–8 | 25–30 | 2–3 | 2–3 | 1 |
| 5 min | 3 | 8–12 | 40–50 | 2–4 | 3–4 | 1–2 |
| 10 min | 3 | 15–20 | 80–100 | 3–5 | 4–6 | 2 |
| 20 min | 4 | 25–35 | 160–200 | 4–6 | 5–8 | 3–4 |
| 30 min | 4 | 35–50 | 240–300 | 5–7 | 6–10 | 5 |
| 60 min | 5 | 70–90 | 500–600 | 6–10 | 8–14 | 8–10 |
| 90 min | 5–6 | 100–130 | 750–900 | 8–12 | 10–16 | 12–14 |
| 120 min | 6–7 | 130–170 | 1000–1200 | 8–14 | 12–20 | 15–18 |

**60 min+ videos:** story ko **chapters** mein bano (har chapter 10–15 min, apna mini-climax). Isse YouTube chapters bhi ban jayenge aur viewer retention achha rahega.

## 2. Act Time Split (percentage of total duration)

| Part | % | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3% | Pehle 5–15 sec mein sabse strong visual / sawaal |
| Setup (Act 1) | 3–25% | Characters, duniya, normal life, inciting incident |
| Confrontation (Act 2A) | 25–50% | Problem badhti hai, pehli koshish |
| Midpoint | ~50% | Bada reveal ya reversal |
| Confrontation (Act 2B) | 50–75% | Sab bigadta hai, "all is lost" |
| Climax (Act 3) | 75–95% | Final face-off |
| Resolution | 95–100% | Ending, twist, ya next-part ka hook |

## 3. Scene Rules

- Har scene mein: **1 location + 1 time of day + 1 goal**. Location ya time badla = naya scene.
- Har scene 3–8 shots ka (dialogue scene zyada lamba ho sakta hai).
- Scene ki shuruaat **establishing shot** (wide) se, phir medium/close-ups.
- Har scene ke end mein kuch badalna chahiye (info, emotion, danger).

## 4. Shot Types (use karne ke liye)

| Code | Shot | Kab use karein |
|---|---|---|
| EWS | Extreme wide | Nayi location / scale dikhana |
| WS | Wide | Scene establish |
| MS | Medium | Normal action, dialogue |
| MCU | Medium close-up | Dialogue, reaction |
| CU | Close-up | Emotion |
| ECU | Extreme close-up | Aankhein, clue, prop |
| OTS | Over the shoulder | Do log baat karte hue |
| POV | Point of view | Character ki nazar se |
| INSERT | Insert | Prop / clue detail |
| AERIAL | Drone | Transition, scale |

## 5. Camera Movements

static, slow push-in, pull-out, pan left/right, tilt up/down, tracking, dolly, orbit, handheld shake, crane up, whip pan, rack focus.

## 6. Transitions

cut, fade to black, cross-dissolve, match cut, whip transition, light flash, sound bridge.

## 7. Voiceover / Dialogue Timing

- Hindi narration: ~**2.2–2.5 words per second**. 8-sec shot = max ~18–20 words.
- Kids content: ~1.8 words/sec.
- Silence bhi likho: `"dialogue": "[silence — only ambience]"`.
