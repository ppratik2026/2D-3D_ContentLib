🎞 AI motion — every picture becomes a real moving clip (free: your GPU or Kaggle / Colab)

Needs the AI motion server (folder local-motion-server: start_ltx.bat on a PC with an NVIDIA GPU, or kaggle_colab.ipynb on a free Kaggle / Colab GPU). Each picture + a motion prompt (+ the voice for lip-sync) goes to the server; the clip comes back and replaces the still picture in the video. Clips are saved in the project.
